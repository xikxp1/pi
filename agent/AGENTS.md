# AGENTS.md

## Task planning

- For tasks with 3+ meaningful steps, multi-file changes, or investigation
  followed by implementation, call `todo` before starting substantive work.
- Use 3-6 concise, outcome-oriented steps.
- Keep at most one step `in_progress`.
- Update the list as steps finish or the scope changes.
- Before your final response, mark completed steps and accurately retain
  any unfinished work.
- Skip the checklist for simple questions and trivial single-step edits.
- Use the `todo` tool, not just a Markdown checklist in chat.
